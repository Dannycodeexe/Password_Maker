The (Password) Forge

Why 3-2-1?

Traditional random passwords are hard to remember and can be easy to guess, leading users to write them down or just being hacked. A 3-2-1 method relies on human entropy so it becomes much harder to guess but less difficult to remember

❌ Bad: P@ssw0rd1 (Easily guessed, predictable substitutions)

✅ Good: GalaxyOrbitNebula88$ (Mathematically secure, highly memorable)
